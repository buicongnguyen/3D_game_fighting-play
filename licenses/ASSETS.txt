KITEFALL reuses the Hoshi Valley environment and Blender models from the
owner's 3D_game_scene project:
https://github.com/buicongnguyen/3D_game_scene

The fighting game adds procedural characters, weapons, drone, fortifications,
campaign logic, controls, interface and synthesized sound.

Original authorship and distribution terms apply to the reused assets. No
additional asset license is asserted here. Third-party software notices are
included in this directory.
