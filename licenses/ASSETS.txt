KITEFALL reuses the Hoshi Valley environment and Blender models from the
owner's 3D_game_scene project:
https://github.com/buicongnguyen/3D_game_scene

The fighting game adds Blender-authored characters, weapons, a kite drone,
fortifications, supplies, a rescue helicopter and explosive props/effects, using the reference project's geometry and
ambient-occlusion toolkit. It also adds campaign logic, controls, interface
and synthesized sound. Editable Blender sources are maintained separately.

Original authorship and distribution terms apply to the reused assets. No
additional asset license is asserted here. Third-party software notices are
included in this directory.
